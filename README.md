# Olist-Brazil-e-commerce-analysis-dashboard
Key Business Insights

## 1. Rapid Growth During 2017–2018
Sales revenue increased steadily throughout 2017 and remained at a consistently high level in 2018, indicating strong platform expansion and customer acquisition during the period.

### Implication:
The company experienced a rapid growth stage and successfully scaled its marketplace operations.

## 2. Revenue Concentrated in Southeastern Brazil
Sales were heavily concentrated in economically developed states such as São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais.

### Implication:
Regional demand is unevenly distributed, suggesting opportunities for targeted marketing and logistics optimization in high-performing regions.

## 3. Credit Cards Dominated Customer Payments
Approximately 78% of transactions were completed using credit cards, significantly exceeding all other payment methods.

### Implication:
Promotional campaigns involving installment plans and credit card partnerships may have the strongest impact on customer conversion.

## 4. Home & Lifestyle Categories Generated the Highest Revenue
Categories such as Home & Bath, Furniture & Decoration, Sports & Leisure, and Gifts accounted for a substantial portion of total sales.

### Implication:
Consumer demand was largely driven by household and lifestyle-related products.

## 5. Customer Retention Remains a Challenge
Most customers made only one purchase, while repeat purchases were relatively rare.

### Implication:
Retention initiatives such as loyalty programs, personalized recommendations, and remarketing campaigns could improve customer lifetime value (CLV).

## 6. Generally High Satisfaction Among Customers
The majority of reviews received the highest rating score.

### Implication:
The platform maintained a positive customer experience, which provides a strong foundation for retention-focused strategies.

## 7. Weekday Sales Outperformed Weekend Sales
Average daily sales on weekdays exceeded weekend sales.

### Implication:
Marketing campaigns and promotional activities may be more effective when scheduled during weekdays.
